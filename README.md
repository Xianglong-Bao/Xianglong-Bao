I'm currently a PhD researcher at **Griffith University** (Brisbane, QLD, Australia).
My research interests are in **brain-computer interfaces** and **medical AI**,
with a focus on physiological signals such as EEG, ECG, and EMG, and on
**biomedical time series** more broadly.

I graduated from **Tianjin University** (Tianjin, China) in 2025. I worked as a
Research Scientist Intern at the **Ping An Technology U.S. Research Institute**
(Silicon Valley, CA, USA), where I focused on AI4Health. 

Previously I worked
on neuro-symbolic AI, specifically ontology reasoning and the decidability
and complexity of logics.

BTW, I'm also a saxophonist 🎷 and a triathlete 🏊🚴🏃. Both are mostly breath
control and endurance. So, apparently, is research.

### Projects

**[ReBrain](https://github.com/Xianglong-Bao/ReBrain)** — a signal-to-signal model
fit on intracranial EEG alone, then frozen and dropped in as a plug-and-play
front-end that re-expresses scalp EEG through the iEEG-learned representation.
No pairing, no fine-tuning, no architecture change downstream.

**[awesome-biosignals](https://github.com/BosonHwang/awesome-biosignals)** — a
curated reading list I co-maintain, covering notable AI research on physiological
signals such as ECG, EEG, PPG, and EMG.

**[TempRuLe](https://github.com/Xianglong-Bao/TempRuLe)** — code for *Explainable
Temporal Knowledge Graph Reasoning via Expressive Logic Rules*, from my earlier
work on neuro-symbolic AI.
